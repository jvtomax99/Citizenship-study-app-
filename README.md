# Citizenship Study

A free study app for the U.S. citizenship (naturalization) civics test, 2025 version:
the 128 official questions and answers, copied word for word from USCIS (form M-1778,
09/25), with Spanish help.

Open it on a phone: https://jvtomax99.github.io/Citizenship-study-app-/

- Study cards that read the question aloud, a daily drill that brings missed questions
  back sooner, and a mock interview by voice that stops at 12 right or 9 wrong.
- Your state's answers (senators, governor, capital), and the 65/20 list.
- Add it to the Home Screen and it works without internet. Progress stays on the phone;
  a backup file moves it to another phone.
- "How to use this app" at the bottom of the first screen is the guide, in English and
  Spanish.

This app is not made by USCIS and is not legal advice. No accounts, no ads, nothing is
sent anywhere.

For whoever works on it: `SPEC.md` (what it does), `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` (the map),
`CREDITS.md` (every picture and font).
