# Art prompts (2026-11-11)

Pictures are rendered once and saved as WebP, so the app shows 3D art without running
3D on the phone. Made with Runway's image tool, 4:3 at 2K (the eagle 1:1 at 1K), then
resized: scenes to 1440 px wide (60 to 80 KB), the eagle to 256 px and 640 px.

Jose asked for it to look like the real Washington, D.C. (2026-11-11), so the scenes
are the true view, not a toy model. Shared style line: "Architecturally accurate,
true-to-life proportions and materials, real geography. Cinematic high-fidelity
realistic 3D render, rich fine detail, soft atmospheric haze and depth. No text, no
letters, no logos, no people."

- **mall-day:** the real view east from the Lincoln Memorial steps at golden hour: the
  Reflecting Pool straight ahead between rows of elms, the World War II Memorial at its
  far end, the Washington Monument in the exact center with its ring of flags, mirrored
  in the pool, the Capitol dome small in the distance behind it; a large American flag
  at the right edge; cherry blossoms in the top left corner; sky from indigo through
  violet and rose to gold; the bottom fifth fades into flat cream `#FFF8F0`.
- **mall-night:** the same view at night, monument and Capitol floodlit, path lights,
  stars, a rose afterglow; the bottom fifth fades into flat navy `#12152B`.
- **eagle:** a friendly 3D cartoon bald eagle, head and shoulders, warm smile, navy
  collar with a tiny gold star pin, on a plain indigo-violet background. (A realistic
  eagle was tried and looked stern; kept out unless Jose prefers it.)

Tried first and replaced: the same landmarks as a clay toy diorama.

A new picture must match these: same style line, same camera height, same fade color.
