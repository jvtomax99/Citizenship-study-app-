# Art prompts (2026-11-11)

Pictures are rendered once and saved as WebP, so the app shows 3D art without running
3D on the phone. Made with Runway's image tool, 4:3 at 2K (the eagle 1:1 at 1K), then
resized: scenes to 1440 px wide (60 to 80 KB), the eagle to 256 px and 640 px.

Shared style line: "Highly detailed 3D render, stylized miniature look with soft matte
clay and painted-toy materials, smooth rounded bevels, rich small details, soft global
illumination, gentle depth of field, premium mobile game illustration quality, charming
and warm. No text, no letters, no logos, no people."

- **mall-day:** a miniature 3D diorama of the National Mall at golden hour, seen from
  slightly above; Washington Monument in the exact center with a ring of tiny flags;
  Lincoln Memorial left, Capitol right; reflecting pool from the foreground to the
  monument; round trees, cherry trees in bloom, lamp posts, paths; sky from deep indigo
  through violet and pink to peach and gold, sun low behind the monument; the bottom
  fifth fades into flat cream `#FFF8F0`.
- **mall-night:** the same diorama at night, monuments floodlit, lit windows, glowing
  lamp posts, starry navy sky with a magenta afterglow; the bottom fifth fades into flat
  navy `#12152B`.
- **eagle:** a friendly 3D cartoon bald eagle, head and shoulders, warm smile, navy
  collar with a tiny gold star pin, on a plain indigo-violet background.

A new picture must match these: same style line, same camera height, same fade color.
